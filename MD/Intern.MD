E-commerce Data Analyst Intern Project: Customer Service Analytics
Project Executive Summary
Over the course of this internship, the analyst will transform raw Customer Service (CS) activity into actionable business intelligence by completing the following objectives:

Onboarding & Discovery: Shadow the CS team and complete advanced Power BI training.

POC Migration: Rebuild an existing manual Power BI dashboard using a newly established SQL Server data source.

Data Engineering: Construct new data collection pipelines to capture manual email volumes and subjective QA audit scores.

Dataset Unification: Merge phone, email, and audit logs into a single, clean "Productivity Dataset."

Advanced Correlation Modeling: Tackle a complex time-series analysis by bridging CS "Wrap-Up Codes" with asynchronous e-commerce sales data.

Stakeholder Dashboarding: Design and deploy role-specific dashboards tailored for Team Leads (daily operations) and Supervisors (yearly reviews and macro trends).

Phase 1: Onboarding, Training & Discovery
Focus: Understanding the business context and technical tools.

Customer Service Immersion (Week 1): Shadow CS representatives to understand their workflow, pain points, and how they categorize calls using Wrap-Up Codes.

Technical Upskilling: Attend Power BI training sessions led by the partnering professor to ensure alignment on best practices and advanced DAX/modeling techniques.

Current-State Review: Analyze the existing manually-exported POC dashboard to understand the UX and phone call data points currently being tracked.

SQL Exploration: Connect to the newly available SQL Server database, map out the schema, and identify where the raw data for the POC lives.

Phase 2: Data Migration & Pipeline Development
Focus: Automating existing reporting and capturing missing data sources.

POC Recreation: Rebuild the foundational POC dashboard in Power BI, replacing the manual data exports with live queries from the new SQL Server.

Email Tracking Pipeline: Design a collection method to accurately log the volume of emails handled by each representative daily.

QA Audit Database: Build a schema and input mechanism for Supervisors to log random audits (grading reps on specific criteria after listening to calls or reviewing cases).

Data Unification: Model these three disparate data streams (automated phone data, piped email data, and subjective audit data) into a cohesive "Productivity Dataset."

Phase 3: Advanced Analytics & Correlation Modeling
Focus: Bridging the gap between reactive customer service and proactive business impact.

The Wrap-Up Code Challenge: Merge CS Wrap-Up Codes with historical sales data to determine how specific customer issues impact or relate to sales volume.

Time-Series Normalization: Develop a methodology to account for the asynchronous nature of the business:

Lag Attribution: Tying a phone call happening today to an order placed a week prior.

Schedule Discrepancies: Normalizing metrics when sales occur 24/7, but CS calls only occur during daytime hours, Monday through Friday.

Phase 4: KPI Formulation & Role-Specific Dashboarding
Focus: Delivering the final products to the end-users.

KPI Development: Analyze the unified Productivity Dataset to draft standard KPIs. The intern will also be tasked with proposing new, innovative KPIs that highlight UX friction points or rep efficiency.

Lead-Facing Dashboard: Build a tactical, day-to-day dashboard.

Focus: Real-time stats, daily rep performance, and immediate coaching opportunities.

Supervisor-Facing Dashboard: Build a strategic, macro-level dashboard.

Focus: Overall department metrics, long-term UX trends, and aggregated data structured specifically to support Yearly Rep Reviews.
